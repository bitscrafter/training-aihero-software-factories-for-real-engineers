# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform: instructors publish video courses, and learners buy and take them.

## Language

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a reason, to get back what they paid for a course; staff approve or decline it.
_Avoid_: Cancel my purchase, Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
Money given back to a learner for a purchase, as the outcome of an approved Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Chargeback**:
A charge the cardholder disputes with their bank, which pulls the money back without going through us. Distinct from a Refund; not handled by Cadence yet.

### Taking courses

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course.

**Cancellation**:
The end of a learner's Enrollment in a course, so they lose access to it.
_Avoid_: Cancel my purchase (for the request), Revocation
